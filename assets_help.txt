VS-05 Asset Packs
=================

VS-05 loads its assets from asset packs installed in your home directory.
Each pack is a folder containing a complete data tree (units/, sectors/,
meshes/, textures/, ...).

The settings app's "Assets" screen lists the packs it finds and lets you
pick the active one.

Getting the Vegastrike assets
-----------------------------

The default "vegastrike" asset pack is available from the VS-05 repository:

    https://github.com/evertvorster/VS-05-lts-Assets

Download the data and extract it so you end up with a folder named after the
pack, containing the data tree. For example, for the standard assets:

    ~/.local/share/vs-05/assets/vegastrike/

Other asset packs can be placed next to it, one folder each:

    ~/.local/share/vs-05/assets/<packname>/

After placing a pack there, open the settings app, go to Assets, select the
pack, and click Save. It becomes the active asset.
