Governance
----------

Vega Strike is a collaboration of developers.

Background
==========

Vega Strike was originally developed by Daniel Horn with the contributions from many others
along the way starting around 1999/2000 with the first release in February 2001.

Please see the [original SourceForge Member List](https://sourceforge.net/p/vegastrike/_members/)for a complete list.
